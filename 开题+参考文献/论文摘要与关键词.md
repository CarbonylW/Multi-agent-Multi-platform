## 中文摘要

随着无人系统技术的快速发展，无人机、无人艇、无人潜航器等异构无人平台在军事领域的应用日益广泛，海空潜三维立体协同作战已成为未来无人作战的重要发展趋势。然而，传统异构无人平台协同方案面临着决策架构矛盾、空间冲突挑战、多Agent管控难题、鲁棒性与扩展性不足等四大核心问题。

本文提出一种大模型驱动的多层多Agent异构无人平台协同控制与任务调度系统。首先，设计了LLM+RAG+传统算法的混合决策架构，通过明确的决策权划分和有机的决策融合机制，既发挥大模型在态势理解、任务分解、应急决策方面的优势，又利用传统算法在路径规划、精确调度方面的实时性和可控性，实现智能性与实时性的平衡。其次，针对海空潜三维立体场景，设计了一套工程化的冲突检测与消解方案，采用2.5D空间建模、层级检测策略、时间窗口优化等技术，实现秒级的冲突检测与自主消解。再次，从架构设计入手，通过严格的层级通信拓扑、明确的角色权限模型、可验证的指令签名机制，系统性地解决单大模型支撑多Agent时的角色混淆、记忆串扰、指令错发问题。最后，设计了多维度的故障检测机制和事件触发的动态任务重规划策略，实现突发状况下的自适应调整与故障转移，并支持广域扩展。

本文构建了一套完整的仿真验证系统，基于Go语言实现高性能后端核心，基于Python实现LLM服务层，通过典型入侵场景、突发故障场景、复杂协同场景的实验验证，结果表明本文提出的系统在任务成功率、响应时间、冲突消解率、系统稳定性等方面均表现良好，验证了方案的有效性和可行性。

**关键词**：异构无人平台；多智能体系统；大语言模型；混合决策；冲突检测；任务规划

## English Abstract

With the rapid development of unmanned system technology, heterogeneous unmanned platforms such as unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs), unmanned surface vehicles (USVs), and unmanned underwater vehicles (UUVs) are increasingly widely used in the military field. Three-dimensional collaborative operations across air, surface, and underwater domains have become an important development trend for future unmanned warfare. However, traditional collaborative solutions for heterogeneous unmanned platforms face four core challenges: decision architecture contradiction, spatial conflict detection, multi-agent governance, and insufficient robustness and scalability.

This thesis proposes a large language model (LLM) driven multi-layer multi-agent collaborative control and task scheduling system for heterogeneous unmanned platforms. First, a hybrid decision architecture combining LLM, RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation), and traditional algorithms is designed. Through clear decision right division and organic decision fusion mechanisms, this architecture leverages the advantages of large models in situation understanding, task decomposition, and emergency decision-making, while utilizing the real-time performance and controllability of traditional algorithms in path planning and precise scheduling, achieving a balance between intelligence and real-time performance. Second, targeting the three-dimensional air-surface-underwater scene, an engineered conflict detection and resolution scheme is designed, adopting 2.5D spatial modeling, hierarchical detection strategy, and time window optimization techniques to achieve second-level conflict detection and autonomous resolution. Third, starting from the architecture design, through strict hierarchical communication topology, clear role permission model, and verifiable instruction signature mechanism, this thesis systematically solves the problems of role confusion, memory crosstalk, and instruction misdelivery when a single large model supports multiple agents. Finally, a multi-dimensional fault detection mechanism and event-triggered dynamic task replanning strategy are designed to achieve adaptive adjustment and failover under emergency situations, while supporting wide-area expansion.

This thesis constructs a complete simulation and verification system, implementing a high-performance backend core based on Go language and an LLM service layer based on Python. Through experimental verification in typical invasion scenarios, emergency fault scenarios, and complex collaborative scenarios, the results show that the system proposed in this thesis performs well in terms of task success rate, response time, conflict resolution rate, and system stability, verifying the effectiveness and feasibility of the proposed scheme.

**Keywords**: Heterogeneous Unmanned Platforms; Multi-Agent Systems; Large Language Models; Hybrid Decision-Making; Conflict Detection; Task Planning
